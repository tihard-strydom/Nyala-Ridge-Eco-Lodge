About Us

Est. 2026 • Waterberg, Limpopo, South Africa

Established in 2026, Nyala Ridge Eco Lodge is a luxury eco-lodge nestled in the heart of the Waterberg Biosphere, Limpopo. Surrounded by unspoiled bushveld, breathtaking ridges, and diverse wildlife, our lodge offers guests an authentic African wilderness experience while remaining committed to sustainable tourism and conservation.

The lodge takes its name from the large population of free-roaming nyala that inhabit the property. These elegant antelope are frequently seen grazing through the valleys, browsing beneath the ancient trees, and gathering around natural waterholes, making them one of the area's most iconic wildlife species.

At Nyala Ridge Eco Lodge, we believe that luxury and conservation go hand in hand. Every aspect of the lodge has been designed to minimise our environmental footprint while providing exceptional comfort.

The Nyala Ridge Experience

Why discerning travelers choose Nyala Ridge Eco Lodge

Luxury Off-Grid Living

Nestled in the malaria-free Waterberg wilderness. Our lodge features luxury suites with private plunge pools and roof solar integration, running completely off-grid with panoramic views over the Waterberg ridges.

Where the Nyala Roam

Nyala Ridge Eco Lodge derives its name from the exceptional number of free-roaming nyala found throughout the reserve. Guests frequently encounter these magnificent antelope while exploring the valleys, ridges and natural waterholes.

Bushveld Fine Dining

Every meal celebrates the flavours of Limpopo. Fresh produce is sourced from local farmers, our organic gardens and sustainable suppliers, creating an unforgettable farm-to-table dining experience beneath the African stars.
