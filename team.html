Strategic Partnerships & Alliances

Collaborating with industry leaders to preserve the Waterberg wilderness

Conservation Partner

Waterberg Biosphere Reserve Trust

We work in close alliance with the UNESCO-declared Waterberg Biosphere Reserve Trust to promote biodiversity conservation, protect endangered species, and maintain sustainable land use across 4,500 hectares of private wilderness.

Agricultural Partner

Castello Boerdery & Local Producers

In partnership with Castello Boerdery and regional organic farmers, our farm-to-table culinary program prioritizes ethically raised, locally sourced ingredients and sustainable agricultural practices throughout Limpopo.

Wildlife & Safaris

Valley Safari Industry Branch

Collaborating with Valley Safari to deliver world-class ethical wildlife experiences, specialized professional guided safaris, and continuous habitat monitoring for species preservation.

Renewable Tech

Solar Tech Africa Solutions

Engineered in partnership with leading green-tech providers to maintain a completely off-grid 100% solar microgrid system with smart energy storage, ensuring zero reliance on fossil fuels.

Community Trust

Limpopo Eco-Tourism Association

An active corporate member dedicated to training, employing, and empowering local communities in rural Limpopo, creating sustainable career pathways in eco-hospitality.

Aviation Partner

Vaalwater Air Charters

Providing seamless private fly-in transfers from O.R. Tambo International Airport directly to the nearby Vaalwater Private Airstrip for executive lodge guests.
